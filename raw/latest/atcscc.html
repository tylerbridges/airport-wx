EVENT TIME: 05/2100 - 06/0500
CONSTRAINED FACILITIES: ZBW

NORTH ATLANTIC ADVISORY FOR 10/05/2026 2100Z to 10/06/2026 0500Z		
		
ALL AIRCRAFT TRANSITING THROUGH BOSTON-MONCTON OR MONTREAL-MONCTON AIRSPACE		
WITH THE INTENTION OF JOINING THE NORTH ATLANTIC TRACK ORGANIZED TRACK		
STRUCTURE (NAT OTS) MUST FILE THEIR ROUTING VIA THE NARS ASSIGNED TO THE		
OCEAN ENTRY POINT THAT CORRESPONDS WITH THEIR INTENDED NAT TRACK.		
THESE NAR ASSIGNMENTS FOR THE NAT TRACKS ONLY ARE PUBLISHED BY NAV CANADA.		
		
NAT OPERATORS SHOULD REFER TO NAT OPS BULLETIN 2023-001 REV 4. SUBMIT ONLY		
ONE RCL TO CZQX 90-60 MINS PRIOR TO OEP. PLANNED OCEANIC ALT/RTE/SPD MAY DIFFER		
FROM RCL. DO NOT CHANGE ALTITUDE WITHOUT AN ATC CLEARANCE.		
OCEANIC CLEARANCE NO LONGER REQUIRED IN EASTBOUND NAT AIRSPACE.		
		
AS PER NOTAM, TALGO & BOBTU ARE UNAVAILABLE. EASTBOUND NAT FLIGHTS ENTERING OR EXITING		
KZWY VIA CZQM SHOULD FILE VIA NOVOK OR JEBBY.		
		
DEPARTURE TRACKS VIA HAPIE ARE NOT AUTHORIZED WHEN W105 IS SCHEDULED OR ACTIVE.
